Privacy policy

SupportPrivacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Lull collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code. Nothing your child does in Lull leaves the iPad.

What stays on the iPad

Lull stores its settings (sensory profile, theme, play length, Bo's colour) and the date it was first opened, used to run the free week. These stay on the device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes the settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We do not see or receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Notifications

If a grown-up turns on "Remind me the day before", Lull schedules one local notification on the iPad. It is created on the device and no information is sent anywhere.

Children

Lull is made for children and is in the App Store Kids category. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. There are no links out of the app or purchases a child can reach; those sit behind a grown-up gate.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
