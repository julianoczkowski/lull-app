Lull Full and the free week

Every game is open for 7 days from the first time Lull is opened. Nothing is charged and no payment details are asked for. After that, Lights and Sort stay free. Tracks, Letters and the extra themes need Lull Full, monthly or yearly.

The theme your child picked during the free week stays available afterwards.

Restore a purchase

Open Lull, hold the Grown-ups button, go to Lull Full and choose Restore purchases. Lull Full works with Family Sharing.

Cancel or change a subscription

On the iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Lull. Or in Lull: Grown-ups › Lull Full › Manage subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Grown-up areas

Settings and purchases sit behind a grown-up gate: hold the ring for three seconds, and for purchases also type a number written in words.
